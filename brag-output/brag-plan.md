# Launch video plan

**What it is:** A life planner (goals, calendar, tasks, money, habits, journal) with an AI coach that reads all of it and changes your plan only when you approve.
**For:** People who keep plans in five different apps and drop them by midweek.
**What sets it apart:** The coach proposes concrete changes as cards; nothing happens until you tap Approve. $1 a month after a 7-day trial.
**Visual hook:** Six app categories slam in one after another, then collapse into "One app."
**Tone:** default (punchy, clean). Dark violet stage, the real app in light mode on a phone.
**Identity:** brand violet #7C5CF6 / #6A41EC, Plus Jakarta Sans (display), Inter (body), ink #0D0B1A.
**Share caption:** see share-copy.txt

## Storyboard (1080x1920, 30fps, 21.5s)
| # | Time | Scene |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0.0 to 2.5 | Hook: "Goals. Calendar. Tasks. Money. Habits. Journal." stack in on the beat, collapse into "One app." |
| 2 | 2.5 to 5.5 | Reveal: logo + "The life planner that plans with you." |
| 3 | 5.5 to 10.0 | Today screen rises. "Always know your next step." Tap Mark done, XP ticks 162 to 167. |
| 4 | 10.0 to 15.0 | AI Coach. "Ask your coach." Question types in, reply with a proposed change. "You approve. Your plan updates." Tap Approve, card turns Done. |
| 5 | 15.0 to 18.0 | Calendar month view card. "Day, week or month." |
| 6 | 18.0 to 21.5 | Outro: logo, "7 days free, then $1 a month." "Start your free week". |

All UI is real screenshots of the running app (test data), animated with simulated taps and typing.
Music: 120 BPM, A minor to C (Am F C G), plucks on the hook words, kick from scene 3, chimes on XP and Approve in key.
